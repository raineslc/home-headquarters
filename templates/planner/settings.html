Reminders are checked every 15 minutes from 6 AM to 11 PM, so they can arrive a few minutes early or late; one due overnight arrives at 6 AM if the plan hasn’t started. Untimed plans count from 8 AM. On Android, install the app or keep Chrome allowed to send notifications. The message (plan title, time and place) is encrypted on its way through the browser’s push service.

Phone app

You’re using the installed app.

On Android, open this site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). Home Headquarters then opens full screen from its own icon. Long-press the icon for Add activity, Tasks and Lunches.

The app always loads your plans from the server; nothing about your family is stored on the phone. Sign out on shared phones.

Preview scope

Family profiles, one-off and repeating activities, linked and repeating tasks, and school lunches are available. Parent invitations, read-only Google calendar connections and an optional family Google calendar are available. Approval-based AI planning is available when configured. Phone reminders are available once turned on below. Automatic Google sync is still being built.

Hosted on Google Cloud Run with a Neon database, with a weekly automatic backup once it’s set up.
