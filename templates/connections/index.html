Connect your account

Each parent signs in to Home Headquarters separately and connects their own Google account. Only the calendars you select below appear for the household.

Choose Event details to share titles, times and locations, or Busy only to share times without details. Events marked private in Google always appear as Busy. Unselected calendars stay hidden.

Connecting is read-only. Home Headquarters can only add events to Google if you also set up the family calendar below, and then only to the calendar it creates.
